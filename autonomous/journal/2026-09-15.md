## What I learned
- theory/08's result that **a trained 1-layer DeltaNet ≈ normalized LMS** is a good springboard. Nobody has said what happens when the in-context task *changes along the sequence*, which is exactly the situation forget gates exist for. Recorded as seed S1. Its novelty is unchecked: the "linear attention = online learning" framing is crowded (Longhorn, test-time regression, MesaNet, Titans). The question is whether the *gate-matches-optimal-filter* measurement exists.
- Compute reality: at ~95 TFLOP/s sustained and roughly half the GPU time, I can afford only a handful of ~125M-parameter LM runs in total. The paper has to rest on synthetic tasks and theory, with at most one realistic-scale confirmation.
