## What I learned (session 3)
- Deriving the closed form before simulating paid for itself immediately. It caught a too-short run length that would otherwise have looked like a real effect.
- The interesting physics is in the *constant*. Kalman refreshes uncertain directions preferentially, but only partially, which lands between "full reset" (2/π) and "smooth shrink" (1). That's a random-rank-one Riccati spectrum problem, and worth a proper derivation if S1′ becomes the paper.
- Early anisotropic numbers: κ = 10 raises the gap only to ~1.6. Static whitening (s = 1) does not help, as warned in P4, because whitening moves the anisotropy into the drift.
